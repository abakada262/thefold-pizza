Privacy Policy
Last updated: October 2026

The Fold Pizza ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information in accordance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173). This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices available to you.

Information We Collect

When you create an account or place an order, we collect your full name, email address, mobile number, and delivery address. We also keep records of your order history and preferred payment method to provide and improve our services.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to process, prepare, and deliver your orders; verify your account and send order confirmations and delivery updates; respond to inquiries and provide customer support; improve our menu, website, and overall service; and comply with applicable laws and regulations. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

Sharing of Information

We share your information only as needed to fulfill your order. For example, your name, contact number, and address are shared with our delivery personnel, and payment details are handled by our payment provider. We may also disclose information when required by law.

Payment Security

GCash payments are processed securely through PayMongo, a payment gateway licensed by the Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas (BSP). We do not store your GCash credentials or full payment details on our servers. All transactions are transmitted over encrypted connections.

Data Retention

We retain your account information for as long as your account remains active. Order and transaction records may be kept longer where required for accounting, tax, or legal purposes. Information that is no longer needed is securely deleted or anonymized.

Under the Data Privacy Act, you have the right to be informed about how your data is processed; access the personal information we hold about you; correct inaccurate or outdated information; object to processing or request deletion of your data; and file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission.

About Us
The story behind The Fold Pizza
The Fold Pizza

The Fold Pizza is a Filipino pizza brand built on the belief that great pizza should be fresh, affordable, and made with love. We offer 11 hand-crafted flavors in sizes from personal 10-inch to massive 36-inch party pizzas.

Our Menu

BBQ Chicken, Hawaiian, Veggies, Jalapeño, All Meat, Pepperoni, Four Cheese, Shrimp, Spinach, Fold Special and Luau Chicken. Available in 10", 12", 16", 20", 30" and 36" sizes.

Celebration Deals

We specialize in celebration pizzas with free custom messages. Perfect for birthdays, anniversaries, graduations, and any special occasion. Combos start from ₱640.

Happy Hour

Every day from 1:00 PM to 5:00 PM, enjoy up to 50% off on 10", 12", 16", and 20" pizzas, and 30% off on 30" and 36" sizes. Dine-in, pick-up, or rider booking via Lalamove or Grab.
