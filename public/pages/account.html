2. Orders & Payment

All orders placed through our platform are subject to availability and confirmation. Payment must be made in full at the time of ordering or upon delivery for cash on delivery orders. GCash payments are processed securely through PayMongo.

3. Delivery Policy

Delivery times are estimates and may vary due to traffic, weather, or other conditions. The Fold Pizza is not responsible for delays outside our control. Delivery fees apply for rider bookings via Lalamove or Grab.

4. Refunds & Cancellations

Cancellations must be made before the order is confirmed for preparation. Once an order is being prepared, cancellation may not be possible. Refunds for valid cancellations will be processed within 5–7 business days.

Privacy Policy

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide when creating an account (name, email, phone number, delivery address) and information generated when you place orders (items ordered, payment method, delivery history).

How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to process orders, communicate order updates, improve our service, and comply with legal obligations. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Payment Security

GCash payments are processed through PayMongo, a BSP-licensed payment gateway. We do not store your payment credentials. All transactions are encrypted and secure.

Data Retention

We retain your account information for as long as your account is active. Order history is retained for accounting and legal compliance purposes.

About Us
The story behind The Fold Pizza
The Fold Pizza

The Fold Pizza is a Filipino pizza brand built on the belief that great pizza should be fresh, affordable, and made with love. We offer 11 hand-crafted flavors in sizes from personal 10-inch to massive 36-inch party pizzas.

Our Menu

BBQ Chicken, Hawaiian, Veggies, Jalapeño, All Meat, Pepperoni, Four Cheese, Shrimp, Spinach, Fold Special and Luau Chicken. Available in 10", 12", 16", 20", 30" and 36" sizes.

Celebration Deals

We specialize in celebration pizzas with free custom messages. Perfect for birthdays, anniversaries, graduations, and any special occasion. Combos start from ₱640.

Happy Hour

Every day from 1:00 PM to 5:00 PM, enjoy up to 50% off on 10", 12", 16", and 20" pizzas, and 30% off on 30" and 36" sizes. Dine-in, pick-up, or rider booking via Lalamove or Grab.
